Parent brush-up

The 10-minute version of each topic, so you can follow the conversation.

Properties of matter & identifying unknowns

Every substance has a set of measurable properties — density, melting/boiling point, solubility, reactivity. Think of them as fingerprints: match the measurements against known values to identify what something is.

Highest leverage

Claim–Evidence–Reasoning (CER)

Claim = the answer. Evidence = the data that supports it. Reasoning = why that data backs the claim. This is how the teacher wants thinking expressed — if Avi can do CER cleanly, most of the grade follows.

Engineering design cycle

Define the problem → prototype a solution → test it → analyze the results → iterate. It’s a loop, not a line — expect to go around more than once.

Physical vs. chemical change

Physical change: phase change or dissolving — same substance, reversible (ice ↔ water). Chemical change: a new substance forms — usually not reversible (burning, rusting).

Forces & motion (Semester 2)

Newton’s three laws (inertia; F = ma; equal and opposite reactions). Velocity is speed with direction; acceleration is how fast velocity changes. Balanced forces → no change in motion; unbalanced → acceleration. Kinetic energy = motion; potential energy = stored.
